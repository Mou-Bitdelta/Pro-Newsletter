02. MARKET INSIGHT
 

What's driving the markets?

 

Hawkish Fed signals set the tone.

 

Hawkish signals from the Federal Reserve dominated market sentiment this week. Minutes from the October meeting showed growing support among policymakers for further interest-rate increases as inflation remained elevated.

 

The US dollar strengthened, pushing gold to a 14-month low, while Bitcoin traded near $63,000. US equities also faced pressure as investors reassessed the outlook for interest rates and technology-sector valuations.

 

What to watch:
• The US dollar and Treasury yield movements.
• Gold's ability to stabilise following its recent decline.
• Bitcoin's response to changing risk sentiment.
• Further signals from Federal Reserve policymakers.

03. TRADING ACTIVITY
What are traders watching this week?

Trading activity remained active across major crypto markets, with market participants responding to price movements, economic developments and changing market sentiment. Bitcoin and Ethereum continued to draw attention, while several altcoins recorded notable activity across the broader market.
